# Preventive Health Checkups for Men in Houston: Age Guide

"I feel fine — why would I go to the doctor?" It's the phrase we hear most often from Hispanic men in Houston — and the most dangerous one. The conditions that most affect men's health — high blood pressure, diabetes, high cholesterol — progress for years **without any symptoms**. By the time you feel them, they may have already damaged your heart, kidneys or eyesight.

The good news: all of them can be caught with simple, quick tests. In this guide we explain which screenings you need at each age, which warning signs you should never ignore, and how to get everything done in Spanish, **walk-in and without insurance**, right here in southwest Houston.

## Why get a checkup if you feel fine?

Precisely because you feel fine. Hypertension doesn't hurt. Early-stage diabetes goes unnoticed. High cholesterol causes no discomfort. These are called "silent diseases" because the first visible sign can be a heart attack, a stroke or kidney damage that can no longer be reversed.

An annual preventive checkup lets you:

- Catch high blood pressure, diabetes and cholesterol **before** they cause damage
- Know your baseline numbers so changes are spotted early
- Check how your kidneys, liver and [thyroid](/en/services/tiroides) are working
- Adjust your habits with real medical guidance, not internet remedies

Catching things early almost always means simpler treatment, fewer medications and a better outlook.

## Conditions a checkup catches early

- **Hypertension:** the "silent killer"; it damages the heart, kidneys and brain without warning
- **Type 2 diabetes:** very common in the Hispanic community; caught early, it can be controlled and even reversed with lifestyle changes
- **High cholesterol:** a leading risk factor for heart attacks in younger men
- **Thyroid problems:** they cause fatigue, weight changes and mood swings
- **Sexually transmitted infections:** many cause no symptoms; [STI testing](/en/services/enfermedades-transmision-sexual) is quick and confidential

If you already live with one of these conditions, ongoing [chronic condition care](/en/services/condiciones-cronicas) prevents complications and hospital stays.

## Warning signs: don't brush them off

See a provider as soon as possible if you notice:

- Chest pain or pressure, even if it goes away on its own
- Excessive thirst, constant hunger or frequent urination
- Fatigue that doesn't improve with rest
- Frequent headaches or blurry vision
- Numbness or tingling in your hands or feet
- Wounds that are slow to heal
- Changes in urination or in your intimate life
- Any new lump, especially in the testicles

None of these signs automatically means something serious — but every one of them deserves a look, not "toughing it out."

## Practical tips for taking care of your health

1. **Get a checkup once a year**, even if you feel perfectly fine — it's a one-hour investment
2. **Know your numbers:** blood pressure, glucose and cholesterol; write them down and compare year to year
3. **Move 30 minutes a day** — walking counts
4. **Watch portions** more than strict diets; cut back on sodas and sugary drinks
5. **Sleep 7–8 hours**; poor sleep raises blood pressure and blood sugar
6. **Don't smoke**, and go easy on alcohol
7. **Talk about your health** — normalizing it at home helps your kids and brothers take care of themselves too
